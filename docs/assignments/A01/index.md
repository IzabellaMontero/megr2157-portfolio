Each assignment is easily accessible directly from this outline without having to search through other pages. Because the assignments are labeled individually and numbered, readers are able to locate a specific assignment in under 60 seconds. 

**Reproducibility:**
   Hernandez document gives enough information for a reader with an engineering background to reproduce most of her work without needing additional clarification in assignment 2. Her calculations are shown step by step using free body diagrams and shows the internal and external forces of the truss. Becuase of the equations, diagrams, and numerical values that are provided, another engineering student may easily follow the same process. 

**Evidence of reasoning:**
   Hernandez provides evidence of her reasoning throuhgout the analysis rather that only presenting the final results. She also explains why she selected she selected 230 MPa as the yeild strength and why the calulated area of 2380.043 mm^2 was rounded up to 2400 mm^2. These explanations allow the reader to understand the reasoning behind her engineering decisions and follow how she reached her final design. 

**Professional Tone:**
Hernandez uses language and formatting that are appropriate for an engineering portfolio intended for an employer by using engineering terminology such as static equilibrium, factor of safety, yield strength, and more. The calculations are accompanied by explanations of the design process, allowing a reader to understand her work.
