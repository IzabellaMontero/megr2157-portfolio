**Navigability:**
   Hernandez's assignments are very well organized in a numbered outline on the right side of the portfolio. Ranging from assignments one to eleven. Each assignment is easily accessible directly from this outline without having to search through other pages. Because the assignments are labeled individually and numbered, readers are able to locate a specific assignment in under 60 seconds. 

**Reproducibility**
   Hernandez document gives enough information for a reader with an engineering background to reproduve most of her work without needing additional clarification. In Assignment two she includes design parameters, dimenions, applied loads, material and support conditions. Her calculations are shown step by step using free body diagrams and shows the internal and external forces of the truss. Becuase of the equations, diagrams, and numerical values that are provided, another engineering student may easily follow the same process. 

**Evidence of reasoning**
   Hernandez provides evidence of her reasoning throuhgout the analysis rather that only presenting the final results. She explains why member FD was selected to determine the minimum cross sectional area, since it experienced the largest force of 182.47 KN. She also explains why she selected she selected 230 MPa as the yeild strength and why the calulated area of 2380.043 mm^2 was rounded up to 2400 mm^2. These explnantions allow the reader to understand the reasoning behind her engineering decisions and follow how she reached her final design.
